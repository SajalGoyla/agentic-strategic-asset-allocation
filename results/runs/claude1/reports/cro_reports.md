A modest downward revision to the short-rate estimate would erase most of the stated Sharpe advantage, and the proposer's own notes say as much.; Benchmark-relative risk: active weight of -60% equity and +60% fixed income, with a tracking error of 9.30%. Such events are small in this asset but cannot be diversified away here.; Estimation sensitivity: the Sharpe ratio is a small numerator over a very small denominator, so it moves a lot with small errors in the risk-free rate or CMA, and the headline ratio should not be treated as precise.
