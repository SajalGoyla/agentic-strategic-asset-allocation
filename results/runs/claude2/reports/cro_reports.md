## Key risks

- **adversarial_diversifier** (score 0.37): Opportunity-cost and return-shortfall risk in short_treasuries: the 100% position cannot reach CPI + 3% and is 60 points underweight equity against the 60/40 benchmark, so in expansion the shortfall shows up as drift from the benchmark rather than as loss.; Reinvestment and inflation risk: the whole portfolio is one short-duration rate exposure. Falling policy rates cut its yield almost immediately, and inflation above the CMA assumption erodes real return with no other asset to offset it.; Single-input dependence: the corner only clears the Sharpe floor because of the judged short-Treasury expected return and its tiny variance. A modest CMA error would change the result, so model risk sits in one number.; Zero diversification: effective N is 1.0, so there is no second source of return or risk. Any issuer, liquidity or policy event in the short Treasury sleeve hits 100% of the portfolio, though that is unlikely for this asset.; Tail risk is understated rather than large: the portfolio takes almost no equity or duration drawdown risk, but the 1993-onward panel has no episode of sharp inflation or an abrupt rate shock for short-duration paper.
- **black_litterman** (score 0.40): Rate/duration exposure: 37.4% intermediate Treasuries plus 9.7% long Treasuries (+17.1% active fixed income vs benchmark on those two lines) is a 47% bond sleeve whose worst episode, the 1970s-80s inflation and rate shock, is outside the 1993+ panel. A stock-bond correlation turning positive (as in 2022) would hit both sleeves at once and is the main route to a drawdown worse than the backtest shows.; Equity style tilt: US large cap is 41 points under benchmark, while value (+13.6%), growth (+6.7%) and small cap (+5.0%) are all overweight. Equity is 14% underweight in aggregate, but the composition is a real style and size bet on top of it. Tracking error is mostly this plus the duration overweight.; Input risk: the weights come from CMAs blended with equilibrium returns built on ETF-size market-cap proxies. The 37% Treasury position and the value/large-cap split are only as good as those inputs. The proposer admits expected return and Sharpe are computed from the same CMAs, so they are not independent evidence.; Real-asset diversifiers: gold (4.4%) and REITs (2.5%) give +6.9% active real assets. Gold's drawdown history before 1993 (1980-2000) is mostly absent, and REITs are rate-sensitive, so they add to rather than offset the duration risk in a rate shock.; Tuned-setting risk: the portfolio is the tangency point moved along the frontier by lowering gamma to reach the volatility floor. It holds more risk than the method would choose on its own, so its exposures are policy-driven and not the method's own optimum.
- **cvar_minimization** (score 0.41): Stock-bond correlation turning positive: 45.4% in us_large_cap and 37.3% in intermediate_treasuries together make up about 83% of the portfolio. The LP picks a 4-asset corner using in-sample scenarios, so the hedge weights are fitted to the specific bad months that occurred. A different crisis mix would give different weights.
